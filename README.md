# TradingView Discord alerts

Sends new top stories from [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/markets/stocks-usa/news/?market=topstories) to a Discord channel, with each headline's sentiment, why it matters, and a link to the full story.

Also posts a short morning brief with a daily market regime reading and the day's key economic events, earnings, dividends and IPOs, plus major economic results and earnings recaps as they're released.

A one-page PDF recap of the week goes out every Friday after the close.

Substack Notes from the people listed in `substack_feeds.json` is posted as preview cards.

AI summaries are only posted when their quotes and numbers match the source article.
