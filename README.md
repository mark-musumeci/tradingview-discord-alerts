# TradingView Discord alerts

Sends new US stock top stories from [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/markets/stocks-usa/news/?market=topstories) to a Discord channel, with the headline and a link to the full story.

Also posts a daily update on economic events, earnings, dividends and IPOs from TradingView's calendars, plus major US economic results as they're released.
